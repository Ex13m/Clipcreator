# BOSSET.CZ: ad direction (assortment, heroes, brand world, concepts)

Date: 19 Oct 2026. Follows BRIEF.md › "ZMĚNA SMĚRU": a product ad in Bosset's own visual world; from TA only the TA CZ plate. Facts come from `research/bosset.md` (incl. Verification) plus the pages listed below, all fetched through the WebHook bridge on 19 Oct 2026. Counts and prices are snapshots from that day.

> **RU summary:** Bosset.cz — e-shop и официальный дистрибьютор Keitech и YGK X-Braid в ČR, собственного производства нет. В магазине 6 302 позиции. Больше всего Select (2 335, 37 %), Keitech (1 480, 23 %) и Brain (771). Мягкие приманки — 2 786 позиций (44 % ассортимента), из них 48 % Keitech. Все 12 самых продаваемых товаров магазина — Keitech Easy Shiner. Отдельная необычная категория — термосы Zojirushi (214 позиций), самый продаваемый — SM-VB 0,6 l Black. Призы MČR — термосы, поэтому герои: **H1 SM-VB 0,6 l Black**, **H2 SM-SR 0,6 l зелёный + макро открытой крышки (DOUBLE LOCK)**, **H3 Keitech Easy Shiner 4" Ice Chartreuse** (ядро магазина; апскейл Higgsfield 4k, 2 кредита). Второстепенные: оранжевый SM-SHE, розовый и белый SM-SR, зелёный и бежевый SM-VB, два макро крышки, катушка YGK X-Braid Upgrade X8. Мир бренда: бирюза логотипа #1B97B1, глубокая бирюза #0A4A58, светло-голубой #C5EBFC из шапки сайта, жёлтый акцент #FFC500 из CSS, чёрный. Шрифт **Saira Expanded Black** (wdth 125, wght 900) — почти копия широкого квадратного «BOSSET» в логотипе; установлен. Рекомендация: **концепт A «Ceny pro vítěze»** (подиум, цветовой ряд, горячо/холодно, счётчик «6 HODIN»), а в Reel на end card одна строка из B: «Oficiální distributor Keitech a YGK X-Braid pro ČR».

### Positioning (summary)
- A **spinning (přívlač) e-shop and distributor** with **no own production**. "MojePřívlač.cz" appears as a 4-item brand and in image file names; the relation is not verified, so do not use it.
- **Price segment:** entry to mid for the volume business (Select soft baits 38–49 Kč, Keitech 189–209 Kč per pack), with a **premium Japanese layer** that the shop itself puts forward: "značek té nejvyšší Japonské kvality - Keitech a YGK X-Braid" (meta). JDM wobblers (Megabass, O.S.P., ZipBaits, Bassday) were not price-checked.
- **Specialities:** official CZ distribution of Keitech and YGK X-Braid, a strong Trout Area section, and the Zojirushi thermos range, which is where the MČR prizes come from.
- **Service claims** (footer banners, 3rd person on screen): "Expedujeme do 24h – Objednávky přijaté do 12h expedujeme tentýž den", "Oficiální distributor Keitech a YGK X-Braid pro ČR", "Vše co je skladem máme opravdu skladem". "O nás": "Lovu přívlačí na umělé nástrahy se věnujeme více než dvacet let".

### Cut-out quality (operator option B)
- **Higgsfield: 2 of 12 credits.** Only H3 Keitech was upscaled (bytedance **4k**, 2 cr, because the source is 900×900, under 1000 px). The thermoses were not upscaled: their sources are 1200–1500 px. `remove_background` was not used. Log: `assets_prep/bosset/hf_log.md`. Upscale: `assets/bosset/hf/hf_keitech_es4_4k.png`.
- **Bottles** (`scripts/assets_prep/bosset_ad/build.py::dark_cut`): the packshots sit on pure paper white (255). The object is darkness > 10, **filled row by row** between the outer edges, so pale highlights cannot bite holes. The 1–2 px rim gets alpha from a darkness ramp against the local rim tone (1-D unmixing) and colour from inside (`edge_clean`), which leaves no white halo. ISNet and `cutout3` bit holes into the pastel and white bottles (S2 cap, S4/S5 body), and **ISNet bleeds onto pure white at the frame edge**, which gave white tabs under the bases. `clean_isnet()` now zeroes ISNet where the photo is paper white.
- **Macros + spool:** `cutout3` (ISNet trimap + white unmixing, from `velkaryba_ad/cut.py`) with cleaned ISNet.
- **Keitech:** the upscaler left white "dust" on the rim. `smooth_cut` builds the contour from ISNet ∪ darkness, blurs and re-thresholds it, pulls it in by 2 px, anti-aliases it, and takes rim colours from 6 px inside. Then a Lanczos downscale to 2400 px.
- QA: `ads/_sheet.jpg` shows dark #0A0F12, light #F4F6F5, **teal #1B97B1** and **light blue #C5EBFC**. Edges were checked in 1.4–3× crops on black and teal. The black SM-VB keeps a thin lighter rim: that is the studio rim light in the original photo, not a halo. No watermarks on any Bosset image.
- `ads/_brandworld.jpg` has 4 mood frames at 1080×1920 with the real TA plate (64, 250, w 300) and the Reels UI zones tinted red. Script: `bosset_ad/moodboard.py`.

## 3. Brand world

| Token | Hex | Source | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **teal** (primary) | **#1B97B1** | logo colour (`logo.svg`, bosset_logo_eshop_bez_pozadi.png) | hero fields, light sweeps, rim glow. White text on it is 3.4:1, so use it **only for large display** |
| deep teal | #0A4A58 | *derived* shade of #1B97B1 (for depth; not on the site) | type background. White 9.8:1, yellow 6.2:1 |
| light blue | **#C5EBFC** | site CSS `--color-header-background` | the light set / podium top. Deep teal on it is 7.8:1; teal on it is only 2.7:1, so don't use teal text |
| yellow (accent) | **#FFC500** | site CSS `--color-secondary` / `--color-primary-hover` | chips and hit words, ≤ 10–15 % of the frame. Black on yellow is 13.2:1. **Never yellow text on #1B97B1 (2.2:1)** |
| black / ink | #000000 · #080C0E | site CSS `--color-primary` | night set; teal on ink is 5.7:1 |
| white | #FFFFFF | — | type on teal / deep teal |
| product accents | SM-VB black #403B40 · SM-SR green #B7DBD4 · SM-SHE orange #EC8C50 / #CA5B31 · pink #E3CDE1 · beige #DFD6C4 · Keitech chartreuse #81ED3F | `ads/manifest.json` (dominant colours) | colour-run fields, hot/cold split (orange vs. green) |

- **Logo:** teal **extended, heavy, squarish** lettering "BOSSET" with rounded-rectangle O, B and S, a rule, and a widely tracked thin "F I S H I N G"; a fish/"B" pictogram knocked out of a teal square. Variants: `logo_on_dark.png` (white mono) on teal, deep teal and black; `logo_on_light.png` (teal) on white and light blue. Never use the teal logo on #1B97B1. Logo rule: never bigger than the TA logo in the same frame (`ctx.logo`, ≤ ~281 px wide at plate scale 1).
- **Typography:** the shop runs the Shoptet template font **Nunito** 300/400/700/900 (`--template-font: "Nunito"`). Nunito is already the world of Activefishing and Velká ryba, so Bosset takes its type from the **logo** instead.
  → **Saira** (variable, wght 100–900 × wdth 50–125): **Saira Expanded Black** (wdth 125, wght 900, uppercase) is close to the extended squarish "BOSSET". **Saira Expanded 400–500 uppercase with +0.3 em tracking** echoes "F I S H I N G" (labels, the "HORKÉ I STUDENÉ" line). Chips and the URL: Saira wdth 110, wght 700–800. Width can be animated (wdth 75 → 125) with `fx.varType` for a kinetic "stretch" on the hit word, like the logo's extended stance. Compared against the logo (Michroma, Krona One, Russo One, Archivo Expanded and Outfit were also tried), Saira was the closest. Orbitron was rejected because it has no latin-ext, so no Czech diacritics.
  → **Installed:** `fonts/saira-{latin,latin-ext}-wdth-normal.woff2` + `LICENSE-saira.txt` (@fontsource-variable/saira 5.3.0, OFL), a copy in `proto/assets/fonts/`, and `@font-face` for 'Saira' (weight 100 900, stretch 50–125 %) appended to `proto/assets/fonts.css`. For the engine the block module declares it itself (BLOCK_API §9, shared `lib/fonts.mjs` untouched):
    ```js
    export const fonts = { Saira: { v: 'saira-{r}-wdth-normal', weight: '100 900', stretch: '50% 125%', axes: ['wght', 'wdth'], use: 'Bosset display (Saira Expanded Black echoes the logo)' } };
    export const theme = { bg: '#0A4A58', bg2: '#1B97B1', fg: '#FFFFFF', accent: '#FFC500', accent2: '#C5EBFC',
      font: { display: 'Saira', text: 'Saira' }, wght: 900, upper: true, light: false };   // headline({ wdth: 125 })
    ```
- **Tone of voice:** practical, service-first and proud of the brands it carries. It uses "we" ("se věnujeme více než dvacet let", "preferujeme nástrahy, které máme osobně vyzkoušené"), plain promises ("Expedujeme do 24h", "Vše co je skladem máme opravdu skladem") and one superlative of its own about the *brands* ("té nejvyšší Japonské kvality"). For the ad: short, confident Czech in which **numbers are the heroes** (6 hodin · 0,6 l · 24 h), with no invented superlatives.

## 4. Concepts (block = bars 37–40, 4 bars ≈ 7,3 s at 129 BPM; VO from 0,25 s: "Ceny pro vítěze věnuje Bosset – termosky, které udrží nápoj horký i studený až šest hodin.")
Block-local word times (words.json + 0,25 s): *Ceny* 0,25 · *vítěze* 0,85 · *Bosset* 1,83 · *termosky* 2,62 · *horký* 4,37 · *studený* 5,03 · *šest* 5,91 · *hodin* 6,24–6,57. Bars at ≈ 0 · 1,83 · 3,67 · 5,50 (use `ctx.bar()` / `ctx.vo.at()` + `snap`, never these numbers).

### A · "CENY PRO VÍTĚZE" (the prize as a trophy, Japanese-product-ad grammar), **recommended**
Set: a teal studio (radial #1B97B1 → #0A4A58) with type on the deep part, the site's light-blue #C5EBFC as podium and second set, yellow only for chips.
1. **Bar 1 · "Ceny pro vítěze věnuje"**: H1 SM-VB black drops onto a light-blue podium step marked "1" (hit on the downbeat, contact shadow plus a short squash in y), and a specular sweep runs down the matte body (`fx.sweep`). **CENY PRO / VÍTĚZE** (Saira 125/900, white + yellow on deep teal) reveals by lines on *Ceny* and *vítěze*.
2. **Bar 2 · "Bosset – termosky"**: on *Bosset*, the logo (on_dark, ≤ TA) sits top right. A whip transition to the **colour run** on light blue follows: S3 → S5 → S2 → H2 → S4 → S1 snap in one per beat around H1 (`fx.productIn` stagger), and the label **TERMOSKY 0,5–0,7 L** (deep teal / ink) lands on *termosky*.
3. **Bar 3 · "které udrží nápoj horký i studený"**: a **macro push-in on H2m** (lid open, DOUBLE LOCK icons) over a split grade: the left half is a warm orange field (#EC8C50 → #CA5B31) and the right half a cool green/teal one. **HORKÉ** lands on the warm side on *horký*, **STUDENÉ** on the cool side on *studený*. Saira Expanded 400 tracked caption: "DOUBLE LOCK · OTEVŘEŠ JEDNOU RUKOU" (allowed with SM-SR).
4. **Bar 4 · "až šest hodin."**: back to H1 centred on deep teal. A huge odometer **6** (`fx.odometer`, Saira 125/900) with a yellow chip **AŽ** before it and **HODIN** after it, landing on *šest*. A last sweep follows, then a `tOut` to the next block colour. In the film the TA plate stays in its corner the whole time.
- Pros: every beat matches the VO word; the prize and its real verified USPs (6 h, one hand, BPA-free) are the story. It is the **only non-lure, lifestyle-product world** in the film, so it does not collide with the five lure sponsors, and teal + light blue + yellow read as Bosset's own site.
- Cons: Bosset's core (Keitech, YGK) is invisible in the film block, so the Reel end card has to carry it (see recommendation).

### B · "ZA CENAMI STOJÍ PŘÍVLAČ" (the shop behind the prizes)
Bars 1–2 show the **shop world** under "Ceny pro vítěze věnuje Bosset": a black set with a diagonal yellow band, the **H3 Keitech Easy Shiner** slashing across it with a light trail, the **S6 YGK spool** spinning in, and the line **OFICIÁLNÍ DISTRIBUTOR KEITECH · YGK X-BRAID PRO ČR**. On *termosky* a hard cut to bars 3–4: the colour run and the **6 HODIN** counter as in A.
- Pros: it shows who Bosset really is (the best sellers and the official distributions), and it pops.
- Cons: in bars 1–2 the picture fights the VO ("termosky" vs. a soft bait). It **overlaps** the five lure-heavy worlds (Troutshop, Activefishing, Moonky, Jigovky, Velká ryba). Third-party brand packaging takes the frame, so it risks reading as a Keitech/YGK ad.

### Recommendation
**A** for the block, plus **one B element in the standalone Reel** so the Reel explains Bosset on its own:
- **Reel hook (1 bar):** H1 drops onto the podium on the downbeat, with **CENY PRO VÍTĚZE** readable at 0,3 s and a yellow chip **AŽ 6 HODIN**. The H2m macro is the alternative if the operator wants a "detail" hook.
- **Block:** concept A as above.
- **End card (2 bars):** deep teal #0A4A58 with a soft teal glow. `logo_on_dark` (≤ TA logo), **bosset.cz** (Saira 125/800), **"Partner MČR v Trout Area 2026"**, then one small Saira-400 tracked line: **"Oficiální distributor Keitech a YGK X-Braid pro ČR"** with the H3 Keitech gliding through below it (a single cameo, ≤ 30 % of the frame). The TA logo is never smaller than the sponsor logo. Optional: "Expedice do 24 h" (the shop's own banner) instead of the distributor line if it is too long at phone size.
- **Layout:** text and logos inside x 64–1016, y 240–1460. No text in the right UI column (x > 940, y > 900). Keep the TA plate area (64, 250, w 300, about 60 px tall) free: headlines start at y ≥ 420, or right of the plate. Macros always bleed off the bottom edge (they are cropped there).

### Copy guardrails
- **Nothing about Zojirushi origin** (no "japonská termoska"). Japanese quality is the shop's claim about Keitech and YGK only. Better not to say "japonský" at all in this block.
- "**až 6 hodin**" (never "více než"; the SM-SF page contradicts itself). "Horké i studené" applies to the 0,6 l bottles.
- "Jednou rukou" / "DOUBLE LOCK" / "téměř jako telefon" may appear **only with SM-SR or SM-VB** visuals, never next to the orange SM-SHE.
- "0,5–0,7 l" is the brief's range. The shop's bottles in that range are 0,6 l (plus the 0,52 l SW-KA food jar, which is not used). "0,6 L" on a specific bottle is verified.
- "Oficiální distributor Keitech a YGK X-Braid pro ČR" (footer banner), not "japonských značek". Don't present Keitech, YGK or Zojirushi products as Bosset's own ("naše termosky" is out; "termosky od Bosset.cz" or "věnuje Bosset" is fine).
- No prices, no item counts, no "nejprodávanější" on screen (internal snapshots of 19 Oct). Don't use "Prodávají jen vyzkoušené" (REJECTED in Verification); the allowed wording is "dávají přednost nástrahám, které sami vyzkoušeli".
- No "nejlepší". Czech NBSP rules: "6 hodin", "0,6 l", "24 h", "v Trout Area".
