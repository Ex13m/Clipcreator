Date: 19 Oct 2026. This follows BRIEF.md › "ZMĚNA SMĚRU": a product ad in Gausse Holler's own visual world, with only the TA CZ plate kept. Facts come from `research/gausseholler.md` (Verification) and the pages below. All pages were fetched through the WebHook bridge on 19 Oct 2026 at about 18:30 UTC, and the numbers are a snapshot from that moment.

> **RU summary:** Gausse Holler — **собственное производство** (Custom Lab, ручная сборка в Чехии), а не магазин. В фиде 16 товаров, 11 из них свои. Из своих **8 — стойки** (семейства Revolut 8 и Snap 8, 7 700–8 900 Kč), ещё 3 — компоненты: Aluminium Set (8 цветов анодирования, 2 400 Kč), набор карбоновых трубок (9 цветов, 1 900 Kč) и одиночная трубка (360 Kč). Остальные 5 товаров — «Bazar z dílny» (б/у снасти владельца). Позиционирование: премиум-ниша, лимитированные серии по 5 номерных штук, кастом-цвета, детали меняются без инструмента. Новые эдиции выходят почти каждую неделю. Герои: **H1 Revolut 8 Black Standard Edition** (3/4, фронт и вид сверху), **H2 Revolut 8 в 7 цветах** (один ракурс, цвета меняются на месте), **H3 Snap 8**. Ещё 6 второстепенных: Alu Set orange/black, карбоновые трубки, Nevergreen, Black & White. Вырезки сделаны локально, Higgsfield не понадобился (0 кредитов). Рекомендую **концепт A «LAB»**, а hook для Reels взять из **B «Colourway drop»**. Риск: VO длиннее блока примерно на 0,8 с, слово «série» обрежется.

### Positioning (summary)
- **A manufacturer, not a reseller.** The shop is a one-person "Custom Lab" that builds its own rod stands for trout area by hand: "Hand-built in Czechia in limited series", "Limited series of five. Each stand is numbered."
- **Price segment: premium niche.** A stand costs 7 700–8 900 Kč. No competitor brands are sold new.
- **Specialities:**
  1. **Colour as a system.** There are 8 anodising colours and editions. The site says "every part is a drop-in replacement, no tools beyond your hands" and, in Czech, *"Vyměňte barvu svého stojanu bez nářadí."*
  2. **Materials.** The stands use carbon twill tubes (Ø 32/30 mm, 200 mm, glossy clear coat), anodised aluminium, printed holders and rubber grips.
  3. **Made to order and prototypes.** In Czech on the custom pages: *"zakázkové stojany vyrábíme na objednávku"*.
- **What dominates visually:** black carbon and matte black aluminium with **one colour accent**: the orange logo on the Black edition, or the anodised legs, hub and cap on the colourways. Every product image is a 2048 px studio render on a graphite gradient.

### Cut-out quality
- **Method (local, no AI):** ISNet silhouette (smoothed), then ghost-lobe peeling, then classifying the see-through gaps between tubes as open or filled, then a studio plate estimated by multi-scale normalized convolution, then colour unmixing in a 3 px band against that plate, then decontamination. Result: no grey halo on light or blue sets, clean tube openings and slots, no floor shadow specks.
- The H1 grip end-cap rim has the same grey as the backdrop (42/42/42), so it is forced with a fitted ellipse (4× supersampled AA).
- Code: `scripts/assets_prep/gausseholler_ad/{cut.py,build.py,sheet.py}`. Run `python3 build.py <isnet_dir>`, with mattes from `scratchpad/bgnode/rmbg.mjs medium`. Raw shop renders: `assets/gausseholler/ads_src/` (shop CDN, 19 Oct).
- **Higgsfield: 0 credits** (log: `assets_prep/gausseholler/hf_log.md`). Every source is a 2048 px render (none under 1000 px), and the local cut-outs have no visible defects. The heroes are 1284–1936 px on the long side. H2 (1365) and H3 (1284) are below 1400 only because the shop renders are framed wide. Do not scale them above `maxUp` 1.3. They fill about 1000 px of the 1080 frame anyway.
- QA: `ads/_sheet.jpg` shows dark #0F1113, light #E9E6E2 and gear blue #1D8FD5, all checked at 100 %. No watermarks on any GH source.
- The old `heroes/h02_revolut8_black_side.png` is a **photo** of a real stand (3D-print layers, pale logo). Do not cut it against the renders.

- **Look:** a dark graphite studio, carbon and matte black aluminium, **one** colour accent per moment: orange on the Black edition, or the anodised colour on a colourway. Ice blue is for small UI and links only. The gear blue lives only in the logo.
- **Typography**
  - The site sets **Inter** everywhere: body 400, subheading 500, headings and accent 700. **H1–H3 are uppercase with letter-spacing 0.25em**, a wide-tracked engineering look (CSS vars `--font-h1--case: uppercase`, `--font-h1--spacing: 0.25em`).
  - The logo wordmark "GAUSSE HOLLER" is a light serif in caps, and "CUSTOM LAB" is a thin geometric sans with wide tracking.
  - Ad set:
    - **Inter 800 uppercase** for big type (tight, −1 % tracking).
    - **Inter 600 uppercase +0.25em** for kickers and labels (exactly the site's heading style).
    - **Cormorant Garamond 500–600** (caps) for the one "crafted" line ("Ručně stavěno v Česku" or the product name). It is the closest OFL match to the wordmark: compared against EB Garamond, Cinzel, Marcellus, Libre Caslon, Spectral and Crimson Pro.
    - **Jost 300 uppercase, wide tracking** for spec micro-labels. It mirrors "C U S T O M  L A B".
  - Installed: `fonts/cormorant-garamond-{latin,latin-ext}-wght-{normal,italic}.woff2` and `fonts/jost-…` (@fontsource-variable 5.3.0, OFL, `LICENSE-cormorant-garamond.txt`, `LICENSE-jost.txt`, copied to `proto/assets/fonts/` as well). `proto/assets/fonts.css` now has the `@font-face` rules. The block module should declare them (no shared-file edit needed):
    ```js
    export const fonts = {
      'Cormorant Garamond': { v: 'cormorant-garamond-{r}-wght-normal', vi: 'cormorant-garamond-{r}-wght-italic', weight: '300 700', axes: ['wght'], use: 'GH wordmark serif (OFL)' },
      Jost: { v: 'jost-{r}-wght-normal', vi: 'jost-{r}-wght-italic', weight: '100 900', axes: ['wght'], use: 'GH "CUSTOM LAB" line (OFL)' },
    };
    ```
  - The placeholder module uses Playfair Display 900 italic. It is too high-contrast and too "fashion" for this brand. Replace it with Cormorant Garamond.
- **Logo assets (existing):** `logo_on_dark.png` (gear + wordmark), `gear_ring.png` + `gear_core.png` (shared 1024 canvas; rotate the ring, with 60° symmetry), `wordline_on_dark.png`, `madeby_on_dark.png`. The logo rule still applies: the sponsor mark is never larger than the TA plate in the same frame.
- **Tone of voice:** a maker's voice. Terse, technical, understated, spec-first, no superlatives: "Hand-built rod stands. Carbon, aluminium, limited series.", "every part is a drop-in replacement, no tools beyond your hands", "Ideas we are testing before they go into production". In Czech: short noun phrases and full stops, the way the VO does it ("Karbon. Hliník. Limitovaná série.").
- Suggested module theme: `{ bg:'#0f1113', bg2:'#16191c', fg:'#e9e6e2', accent:'#e8925a', accent2:'#7fb2c7', font:{ display:'Inter', text:'Inter' }, wght:800, upper:true, light:false }`.

## 4. Concepts
The block is bars 41–44: 4 bars ≈ **7.26 s** (beat grid) at 129 BPM, 1 bar ≈ 1.83 s. VO words (block-local, +0.25 s): Gausse 0.25 · Holler 0.70 · stojan 1.37 · **Black 2.32** · Edition 3.17–3.76 · **Ručně 3.88** · Česku 4.81 · **karbon 5.33** · **hliník 6.02** · **limitovaná 6.66** · série 7.37–7.87.
⚠ **The VO (7.81 s + 0.25 s offset) runs about 0.8 s past the block end, so "série" will be cut** (`!! VO … CUT`). The operator has to choose: re-time or trim the VO (for example drop the first "–" pause, or regenerate it a little faster), or let the line land in the finale's first beat. Keep the last on-screen word ("LIMITOVANÁ SÉRIE") visible from about 6.6 s so the message still reads.

### A · "LAB" (dark-studio product film, Apple / Shimano-Japan grammar), **recommended**
Set: graphite #0F1113 → #16191C, one cold top-rim light, a hairline floor (#282D33) with a soft reflection. Type: Inter 800 caps (bone), Inter 600 +0.25em kickers (ice), one Cormorant line.
1. **Bar 1 · "Gausse Holler"** H1c **top view** in the dark: the radial star is lit only by a rim sweep and turns slowly (about 20° over the bar, ramped into the downbeat). The kicker **GAUSSE HOLLER · CUSTOM LAB** is set in Jost 300 tracked. The gear ring (`gear_ring`) can spin in sync, small and under the TA-plate size.
2. **Bar 2 · "stojan na pruty Black Standard Edition"** Whip to **H1 3/4**: it rises with a floor reflection and a specular sweep across the carbon (`fx.sweep`). Then a **macro push-in to the orange gear cap** on "Black" (2.32 s; push-in ≤ 1.3× on the 1316 px asset). Headline **BLACK STANDARD EDITION** in Inter 800, with "Revolut 8" as a small label.
3. **Bar 3 · "Ručně stavěný v Česku"** Cut to **H1b front** (symmetrical monolith). The Cormorant line **Ručně stavěno v Česku** reveals word by word on the VO. On the downbeat, a chip **8 karbonových tubusů** and a thin spec line **Ø 32 mm · 200 mm** (S4 tube slides in at the edge).
4. **Bar 4 · "karbon, hliník, limitovaná série"**
   - "karbon": a 0.5 s macro plate of the **S3 twill**.
   - "hliník": an **explode** of S1 (orange alu set parts fly into place). Then **colourway flash**: H2 flips through 7 colours on 8th notes while the background tints to each accent at 12 %.
   - "limitovaná": back to H1 Black with a sticker **SÉRIE 5 KS · ČÍSLOVÁNO** (orange #E8925A on graphite) and **gausseholler.com** in Inter 600 caps.
- Pros: it follows the VO word by word, keeps the brief's hero (the Black SE) in focus, and fits the brand's own render look and tone. It is the only **graphite and carbon** world in the film, with no conflict with Velká ryba navy, Troutshop black and red, or Bosset.
- Cons: dark on dark needs light. Every product shot needs a rim light or sweep, otherwise black stands sink into the set. Mitigate with a soft radial lift behind the product (#2a3036 → #0f1113) and the floor reflection.

### B · "COLOURWAY DROP" (sneaker-drop grammar, Nike / On)
The H2 stand stays locked in frame. Its anodised colour **and the whole background field** flip on every beat (blue → orange → red → purple → tiffany → light green → bronze), with oversized outline type **KARBON. HLINÍK.** behind. The Black SE lands as the "drop" on "Black Standard Edition": hard cut to graphite, sweep, logo cap macro. Bar 4 is a grid of all 8 colourways (7 + the Black) with the sticker "SÉRIE 5 KS".
- Pros: a scroll-stopper made for Reels, and it sells the brand's real differentiator (colour as a system, editions).
- Cons: it shouts. The tone is less "lab". The VO talks about the Black edition and its build, not colours, so the colour story competes with the voice. Full-field colour flips also sit close to Moonky's and Velká ryba's colour-pop beats.

### Recommendation
**A for the block, with B as the Reel hook.**
- **Reel hook (1 bar):** H2 colour flips on 8th notes (7 colours, field tinted), landing on the Black H1 at the bar end. Headline **RUČNĚ STAVĚNO V ČESKU** (Inter 800 caps), with **gausseholler.com** in small type. It reads at 0.3 s and is the most colourful frame of the brand.
- **End card (2 bars):** graphite with `logo_on_dark` (gear + wordmark, ring rotating), **gausseholler.com**, "Partner MČR v Trout Area 2026" and the TA logo, never smaller than the GH logo. An optional ghost line "Karbon · hliník · limitovaná série" in Jost 300 at 30 %.
- **Layout:** text and logos stay inside x 64–1016 and y 240–1460. Keep the TA plate (64, 250, w 300) clear: headlines start below y ≈ 420. No text in the right UI column (x > 940, y > 900). Products may bleed there.
