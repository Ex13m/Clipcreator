# ACTIVEFISHING.CZ: ad direction (assortment, heroes, brand world, concepts)

Date: 19 Oct 2026. Follows BRIEF.md › "ZMĚNA SMĚRU": a product ad in Activefishing's own visual world. Only the TA CZ plate is kept from our style. For lure shops, show many lures: at least 6–8 items plus 1–2 hero shots. Facts come from `research/activefishing.md` (Verification) and the pages listed below. All pages were fetched through the WebHook bridge on 19 Oct 2026. Every count is a snapshot from that day.

> **RU summary:** Activefishing is a narrow Trout Area specialist and a reseller. It has no brand of its own. About 80 % of the core range is hard micro-lures: **1 340 spoons + 777 crankbaits**. **Nories** leads the spoons (348 = 26 %). **Timon** leads the crankbaits (269) and runs through the whole shop: 188 spoons, 269 crankbaits and 5 of the 8 rods, 462 items in total. **Mukai** is close behind in crankbaits (267). Lines are **100 % VARIVAS** (159 items), and the homepage pushes the brand ("Prozkoumat značku" → /varivas). Spoons cost 100–180 Kč, and 70 % weigh ≤ 2 g, so these are true area micro-spoons. Crankbaits cost 160–549 Kč, and the rods are premium Japanese at 4 979–13 999 Kč. Heroes: **Timon Chibi Futten FS Tropical Chart Pink** (crankbait), **Nories Sofia SK017 Hot Mango** (a pair of spoons, in the logo's colours) and **VARIVAS ES2 Ester** (line). There are 8 secondary items, so 11 lures from 6 brands in total. Higgsfield used 4 credits of 12. Brand world: logo orange gradient #E39025→#DF5942, site accent #F28A27, black #000 and white. Typography: **Saira Extra Condensed 900** echoes the logo lettering, and **Nunito** is the site font. Recommendation: **concept B "COLOUR DROP"**, a bright white world with an orange counter beat. It is the only light block between dark Troutshop and dark Moonky.

### Positioning (summary)
- A **Trout Area specialist reseller**. There is **no own brand or production** (also stated in activefishing.md). The copy must never imply that Activefishing makes Timon, Nories, Mukai or VARIVAS: use "v nabídce" or "u nás najdete".
- **Hard micro-lures dominate.** Spoons and crankbaits make up 2 117 of the ~2 567 items in the five core categories (≈ 82 %). Soft baits, lines and rods are supporting categories.
- **The range is mostly Japanese-brand**: Nories, Timon, Mukai, ValkeIN, Forest, Rodio Craft, VARIVAS. It is not exclusively Japanese (Libra Lures, FishUp, Boroda Baits). Never say "jen japonské".
- **Price segment: mid to premium** for the category: 100–180 Kč per spoon, up to 549 Kč per crankbait, and premium rods. **Timon is the brand backbone** across three categories (462 items), and **VARIVAS is the only line brand** (159 items plus the homepage brand banner).
- **Personal, founder-run shop** (Stanislav Lukášek, first-person copy on /o-nas/). Homepage USPs: **RYCHLOST DORUČENÍ · UNIKÁTNÍ SORTIMENT · OSOBNÍ PŘÍSTUP**.
- Overlap with the other sponsors: Troutshop also sells Nories and Velvet Arts. Its ads use Nories Rooney and Crankin Pupa, Velvet Arts Forte 2,1 g and Forest Miu, so I avoided those exact SKUs. Jigovky uses a Varivas nylon as a secondary item, while here VARIVAS is a hero (ES2 Ester), which is a different SKU.

### Cut-out quality (operator option B)
- **Higgsfield, 4 of 12 credits.** Bytedance **4k** upscale of H1 and H2. Their shop originals are only 800×600 (the lure is ≈ 700 px and each spoon ≈ 300 px), and the shop's "big" 1024×768 files are only Shoptet's own enlargement of those. 4k costs the same 2 credits as 2k. The first Sofia job failed and was refunded automatically, and the retry succeeded. `remove_background` was not used. Log: `assets_prep/activefishing/hf_log.md`. 4k sources: `ads/_src/*_hf4k.png`.
- **Local cut-out** (`scripts/assets_prep/activefishing_ad/cut_af.py`, built on `velkaryba_ad/cut.py::cutout3`): an ISNet trimap with colour unmixing against the pure-white (255) paper. Three additions:
  - `open_white` re-opens the line-tie eyes, the hook-hanger eyes and the spoon holes that the "enclosed = opaque" rule had filled with paper white.
  - `decontaminate` gives edge pixels the colour of the nearest solid interior pixel and chokes the alpha by 8 %. This removes the light halo left by the upscaler haze; it was checked at 100 % on brand orange.
  - `fill_enclosed` (VARIVAS) restores the white label print and the letter counters, which a white key would delete. Only the see-through lower flange stays transparent.
- **Checked at 100 %:** hook points and barbs are intact, the split rings are open, and there are no halos on orange, white or black. No watermarks: the activefishing.cz packshots are clean.
- **Known limits:** the clear lips (S4 hologram, S7 Mukai) are milky-opaque, as in the packshot. S5 Forte is soft, so use it small or in motion. On pure black, S2's black tail and all black hooks disappear: use a graphite floor (#141414+) or a rim light.

- **Typography**
  - The site loads **Nunito** (Shoptet font-face): headings 700 at 46 px in the hero, buttons 700, prices 22 px.
  - The **logo lettering** is an ultra-condensed heavy sans with rounded-square forms ("FISHING" sits reversed in a white rounded box).
  - Closest OFL match: **Saira Extra Condensed 900** (see the side-by-side test against the logo, Anton, Oswald and Archivo).
  - Ad set: Saira Extra Condensed **900 uppercase** for numbers, labels and short headlines (it echoes the logo). **Nunito 800–950** for sentence lines, pills and the URL (the site's voice: rounded and friendly, "osobní přístup").
  - Installed: `fonts/saira-extra-condensed-{latin,latin-ext}-{700,800,900}-normal.woff2`, `fonts/_ttf/*.ttf` and the same woff2 in `proto/assets/fonts/` (@fontsource/saira-extra-condensed 5.3.0, OFL, `LICENSE-saira-extra-condensed.txt`). `@font-face` rules were appended to `proto/assets/fonts.css`. For the v5 engine, declare the family in the block module, because `lib/fonts.mjs` is not edited:
    `export const fonts = { 'Saira Extra Condensed': { s: { 700: 'saira-extra-condensed-{r}-700-normal', 800: 'saira-extra-condensed-{r}-800-normal', 900: 'saira-extra-condensed-{r}-900-normal' }, use: 'Activefishing display (logo lettering echo)' } };`
  - Why not Anton: Troutshop, the previous block, already uses Anton condensed uppercase. Saira's rounded-square forms come from the logo and read differently.
- **Logo**
  - Use `logo_on_dark.png` / `logo_on_light.png` (the same artwork: an orange fish with white lettering, works on white, grey and black). On the brand-orange field it disappears.
  - New: `ads/logo_white_knockout.png`, a white fish with the lettering knocked out, for orange fields. It is a **derived variant, so confirm it with the sponsor**. Without confirmation, keep logo moments on white or black.
  - Logo rule: the sponsor logo is never larger than the TA logo in the same frame (≤ 281 px wide at plate ×1).
- **Graphic motifs from the site:** pill buttons (radius 30, orange fill or a 2–3 px orange outline), thin 2 px orange vertical frame lines around hero text, and a dark photo hero with a top-down black gradient.
- **Tone of voice:** personal and founder-first ("Mým cílem je…", "Jmenuji se Stanislav Lukášek…"), enthusiastic but plain, with the passion for spinning and trying new lures. Put it on screen as short Czech statements and the three USPs. No superlatives the shop cannot prove.

## 4. Concepts (block = bars 17–20, 4 bars ≈ 7.34 s at 129 BPM; bar starts 0 / 1.83 / 3.67 / 5.50 s)
VO (block-local, +0.25 s offset): "Activefishing (0.25) – e-shop (1.43) zrozený (1.95) z vášně (2.59) pro přívlač (3.20). Přes (4.08) 1 300 (4.34–4.73) plandavek (5.12) a osobní (6.02) přístup (6.51)."

### A · "ORANGE HOUR" (black studio, logo-orange light)
Set: black #000 → a warm radial glow in the logo gradient, rim-lit products, an oversized faint "PŘÍVLAČ" in Saira EC behind, and the site's hero pill (2–3 px #F28A27 outline on a dark glass chip).
1. **Bar 1:** H1 rises out of black as a rim-lit silhouette, then a light sweep over the chart-pink body and a macro push-in on the eye and lip. The headline **ZROZENÝ Z VÁŠNĚ PRO PŘÍVLAČ** builds word by word on the VO, with "VÁŠNĚ" in orange.
2. **Bar 2:** the colourway carousel (H1 → S1 → S3 → S2 → S4) whips on the beats, with the chip "TIMON · CHIBI FUTTEN FS · 51 MM".
3. **Bar 3:** an odometer **1 300+** in orange-gradient numerals on black, with "PLANDAVEK" at 5.12. The spoon fan (H2, S5, S8, Khor, Forte 0,6) fans out with rim light.
4. **Bar 4:** H3 VARIVAS tilts in, then the pill **OSOBNÍ PŘÍSTUP** (6.02), and the logo with activefishing.cz.
- Pros: premium "Japan-reel-ad" grammar, and it mirrors the site hero (dark photo, orange frame lines, orange-outline pill). The orange products glow.
- Cons: it sits **between Troutshop (#070707 + red, Anton condensed caps) and Moonky (navy)**, so it makes three dark blocks in a row with similar condensed-caps type, and Activefishing loses its identity in the film. Black hooks and S2's tail disappear.

### B · "COLOUR DROP" (white studio, orange counter beat), **recommended**
Set: the site's white body (#FAFAF9 → #E8E7E4 floor gradient), real contact shadows and soft reflections, orange used as light and as one full-bleed beat. Display type is Saira EC 900 (orange gradient numerals), lines are Nunito 900 in #111, and the pills are the site's orange buttons.
1. **Bar 1 (0–1.83) "Activefishing – e-shop":** a cold open on an extreme macro of H1's eye and lip sliding in with motion blur, then a specular sweep at 0.25. A speed-ramp pull-back reveals the whole lure floating over its shadow. A small label **ČESKÝ E-SHOP PRO PŘÍVLAČ A TROUT AREA** appears at 1.43 (verified one-liner).
2. **Bar 2 (1.83–3.67) "zrozený z vášně pro přívlač":** a **colourway drop**. The same Chibi Futten switches colourway on every beat, in place and in the same pose (H1 → S1 cobalt → S3 gold → S2 red/black → S4 hologram), with a matching light tint on the backdrop each time, as in sneaker colourway ads. The kinetic headline **ZROZENÝ Z VÁŠNĚ PRO PŘÍVLAČ** follows the VO, with "VÁŠNĚ" in orange at 2.59. Chip: "TIMON · CHIBI FUTTEN FS · 51 MM".
3. **Bar 3 (3.67–5.50) "Přes 1 300 plandavek":** a hard cut on the downbeat to a **full-bleed logo-orange gradient** with an odometer **1 300+** in white Saira EC 900 (digits land at 4.34–4.73) and **PLANDAVEK** at 5.12. The spoon fan bursts radially out of a white rounded "product card" (radius 30, like the site's cards): H2 Sofia in the centre, then S5 Forte, S8 Scheila, Khor and Forte 0,6. The white card keeps the orange and mango spoons readable on orange. A small spec line names brands from the shop filter: "Nories · Timon · ValkeIN · Yarie · Velvet Arts".
4. **Bar 4 (5.50–7.34) "a osobní přístup":** back to white. The H3 VARIVAS spool tilts in in 3D, with S7 Mukai and S6 Micro PaniCra at its sides as a mini grid. The orange pill **OSOBNÍ PŘÍSTUP** lands on "osobní" (6.02), followed by the outline pills **RYCHLOST DORUČENÍ · UNIKÁTNÍ SORTIMENT**, then `logo_on_light` (≤ TA logo) and **activefishing.cz** in Nunito 900.
- Pros:
  - **The only bright block in the middle of the film**, so it gives a clean visual reset after Troutshop's black and red and before Moonky's navy.
  - It is authentically the shop: a white site with orange buttons and Nunito, and a fish logo that works on white without a plate.
  - True product colours, and many lures on screen (11 SKUs, 6 brands), which matches the "HODNĚ nástrah" brief.
  - The orange beat makes the 1 300+ number unmissable.
- Cons: white packshot worlds can feel like a catalogue. It needs real motion craft: macro, speed ramps, shadows and reflections, a parallax depth stack, and beat-cut colour swaps. Orange products on the orange beat need the white card or a cobalt counterpoint.

### Recommendation
**B**, with the Reel framing below:
- **Reel hook (1 bar):** white set. The H2 Sofia pair flicks in with rotation and motion blur, behind it **1 300+** in the orange gradient (Saira EC 900) and **PLANDAVEK** in #111, and the sub "activefishing.cz". It reads at 0.3 s. Equivalent default: `meta.hook = { product: <H2>, headline: 'Přes 1 300 plandavek', sub: 'activefishing.cz' }`.
- **End card (2 bars):** white, using `logo_on_light` (the original artwork, no approval needed), **activefishing.cz**, "Partner MČR v Trout Area 2026", and the TA logo, which stays ≥ the sponsor logo. Optional orange variant with `logo_white_knockout` only after the sponsor confirms it (see `_brandworld.jpg`, frame 3).
- **Theme suggestion:** `{ bg: '#f4f3f1', bg2: '#ffffff', fg: '#111111', accent: '#f28a27', accent2: '#df5942', font: { display: 'Saira Extra Condensed', text: 'Nunito' }, wght: 900, light: true, upper: true }`. Use the orange gradient #E39025→#DF5942 as a field only for the counter beat.
- **Layout:** text inside x 64–1016 and y 240–1460. In the right UI column (x > 940, y > 900) only product edges may appear, no text. The TA plate (64, 250, w 300) stays free, so headlines start at y ≥ 420 or to the right of the plate. The sponsor logo goes top right at ≤ 270 px.

### Copy guardrails
- "Přes 1 300 plandavek" (1 340 on 19. 10.) and "777 woblerů" are **live counts**, so recheck them before the final render. Use "včetně předních japonských značek", never "jen japonské" (Verification #3).
- No "vlastní značka", "naše plandavky", "vyrábíme". Brands are resold: write "v nabídce" or "u nás najdete". VARIVAS is "u nás", not owned.
- No prices on screen (they change and are not in the brief). Spec chips come only from the product names: 51 mm, 1,2 g, 35 mm, 20 mm, 1,8 g, 80 m.
- The homepage quote is in the first person. In VO and on screen, use the third person or the USP words.
- Czech typography: NBSP in "1 300" and after one-letter prepositions ("z vášně", "a osobní").
