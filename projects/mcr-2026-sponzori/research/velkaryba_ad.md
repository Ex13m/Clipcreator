# VELKA-RYBA.CZ: ad direction (assortment, heroes, brand world, concepts)

Date: 19 Oct 2026. Follows BRIEF.md › "ZMĚNA SMĚRU": a product ad in Velká ryba's own visual world, with only the TA CZ plate kept. Facts come from `research/velkaryba.md` (Verification) plus the pages listed below. Every page was fetched through the WebHook bridge on 19 Oct 2026, and the numbers are snapshots from that day.

> **RU summary:** Velká ryba is a retailer that sells spinning gear only (přívlač). It has no brand of its own. Its stock is dominated by **soft baits (1 493 items)** and the **trout program (1 138)**. At the premium end it is **purely Shimano**: the 24 most expensive reels are all Shimano, the most expensive rods are Shimano and G. Loomis, and 23 of the 24 most expensive trout reels are Shimano. The shop is a Shimano Rod Test Center partner. Heroes: **Shimano Vanquish FA Competition Edition** (reel), **Shimano Cardiff Roll Swimmer CE** (Trout Area spoon), **Illex Native Spoon** (the most expensive and one of the largest trout-spoon lines). The Exlead rod plus 6 lures are secondary. Brand world: navy #183C6B / #0F2541, white, and a red #DA0F00 accent used sparingly. The typeface is **Nunito**, the site's own font: 900 italic uppercase for display, 700 for text. Recommendation: **concept A "Shimano speciálka"**, with a Trout Area spoon in the Reels hook.

### Trout spoons (1722, all 169 items)
- Brands: **Shimano 46** (the Cardiff Search, Slim, Roll and Wobble Swimmer lines), **Lucky John 46** (EOS), **Illex 45** (Native Spoon), Westin 11 (Fidusen Slim), Daiwa 8 (Presso ADM), Vanpla 8, Mikado 5.
- Prices run from 35 to 229 Kč, with a median of 129 Kč. Illex Native Spoon is the most expensive line (179–229 Kč). Shimano Cardiff costs 99–139 Kč and Lucky John EOS 81–98 Kč.
- Shimano, Lucky John and Illex make up about 80 % of the category.

### Rods and reels: the premium end is Shimano
- **Reels:** the 24 most expensive are **all Shimano**: Stella SW D (up to 25 599 Kč), Stella FK, Calcutta Conquest DC, Exsence B and Antares B. The cheapest reels start at 999 Kč (Shimano Catana FE). Below that the list is spare spools and oils.
- **Rods:** they range from 1 099 Kč (Shimano STC BB, Favorite U1) to 15 999 Kč (Shimano Exsence Infinity). The 24 most expensive are Shimano (Exsence Infinity, 25 Exsence Genos, 26 Poison Glorious, 26 Lunamis) and **G. Loomis** (NRX+, GLX). The kids' sets at 549 Kč are DAM.
- **Trout reels (29):** 23 of the 24 listed are Shimano: Vanquish FA Competition Edition (11 499–11 799 Kč), Vanquish FC, Cardiff XR, Vanford FA, Ultegra FD, Miravel and Nasci FD. The only other one is a Daiwa 25 Ballistic Air LT.
- **Trout rods (71):** G. Loomis GLX Trout Series (10 499 Kč), Shimano Soare XR (8 599 Kč) and **Shimano Cardiff Exlead Competition, 6 models at 7 999 Kč**, then Favorite Black Swan, G. Loomis GCX Lite and Major Craft Finetail.
- Context for the copy: the shop's blog says *"Jak ostatně už Trout area scéna dobře ví, tak já bez SHIMANA prakticky ani nechytám."* (Cardiff Exlead article, see velkaryba.md). The shop is a **Shimano Rod Test Center** partner (O nás, verified).

### Positioning (summary)
- A **retailer and spinning specialist** ("pouze na přívlač"), with an e-shop and a store in Olomouc (Rooseveltova 127/34) since 2011. It has **no own production or brand**. All heroes are third-party products, so the copy must never imply Velká ryba makes them.
- **Price segment: mid to premium with a long tail.** There are entry products, but the range and the communication are built around premium Japanese gear: Shimano, G. Loomis (a Shimano group brand), Major Craft, Illex.
- **Specialities:** the trout program (1 138 items) and a strong soft-bait catalogue (1 493). Libra Lures Turbo Worm was "made at the request of Trout Area competitors" (velkaryba.md #7). The Lov pstruhů manufacturer filter lists **Jigovky.cz**, another MČR sponsor, which is a possible cross-link but not one for the copy.
- Service USPs in the site footer: "Vše skladem i v prodejně", "Rychlá expedice objednávek", "Výdejní místa po ČR i SK", "Doprava od 59 Kč", "Věrnostní program".

### Cut-out quality (operator option B)
- **Higgsfield (6 of 12 credits):** a bytedance 2k upscale of the three heroes, because their sources are under or at 1000 px (Vanquish 861×1000, Cardiff 1000×748 with the front spoon only ~560 px, Illex 894×1000). `remove_background` was not used: the local cut-out was cleaner. Log: `assets_prep/velkaryba/hf_log.md`. 2k sources: `assets/velkaryba/hf/`.
- **Local cut-out** (`scripts/assets_prep/velkaryba_ad/cut.py::cutout3`): an ISNet trimap plus **colour unmixing against the paper white**, so there are no white halos and the hooks keep their barbs. Topology rule: everything enclosed by the product stays opaque, and only paper-white holes are opened (line-tie holes, the split-ring eye). Contact shadow: an elliptical `_shadow.png` at 1.4× the product width. `fx.product` has its own shadow, so these are optional.
- **Upscaler fixes:** on the Vanquish, the two rotor torx screws came out as invented "grille" domes, so they were patched back from the original. On the Cardiff, the embossed "SHIMANO" is faithful: the stylised logotype reads like "SUIMNNO" in the original too. For macro push-ins, use the spool ("Vanquish CE", "C2000S") and the handle. The screws are fixed but not detailed.
- QA: `ads/_sheet.jpg` shows dark, light and brand navy, all checked at 100 %. `ads/_brandworld.jpg` has 3 mood frames (Nunito 950 italic, navy and the products) laid out in the Reels safe zone. The TA plate is a placeholder at the real position, top left (64, 250, w 300).
- No watermarks: the velka-ryba.cz packshots are clean, and the BACK/FRONT labels were removed by component filtering.

- **Typography:** the site loads **Nunito** 300–800 from Google Fonts (`<link …family=Nunito:wght@300;400;500;700;800>`), and the theme CSS is mostly bold or 600 and **uppercase** (49× `text-transform:uppercase`). The logo's lettering is an italic brush script.
  → Ad set: **Nunito 900–950 italic, uppercase** for headlines (rounded, sporty, and it echoes the script's slant). Nunito 700–850 upright for chips and the URL. Nunito 800 uppercase with +6 % tracking for labels.
  → Installed: `fonts/nunito-{latin,latin-ext}-wght-italic.woff2` plus `_ttf/*.ttf` (@fontsource-variable/nunito 5.2.6, OFL, `LICENSE-nunito.txt`). The Nunito entry in `scripts/lib/fonts.mjs` now has `vi` (the italic is emitted by `fontCss()` and measured by fontkit). `proto/assets/fonts.css` now has `@font-face` rules for normal and italic.
  → Note: Activefishing also uses Nunito, upright. Velká ryba keeps the **italic 900** as its signature, and the two blocks are not adjacent (5 and 10). If more contrast is wanted, the fallback display font is Oswald 700, the placeholder's choice.
- **Logo:** `logo_on_light.png` / `logo_plate.png` (existing). New: **`ads/logo_reverse_on_navy.png`**, with the charcoal fish lines and tagline recoloured white so it works on navy or dark backgrounds without a plate (checked on #183C6B). It is a derived variant, so confirm with the sponsor if it matters. Logo rule: the sponsor logo is always smaller than the TA logo in the same frame.
- **Tone of voice:** a personal, practitioner's voice ("jsme hlavně rybáři", "hrdým partnerem", the blog in the first person: "já bez SHIMANA prakticky ani nechytám"). Straightforward and gear-literate, not salesy. Copy for the ad: short imperatives and facts in Czech, with no superlatives the shop cannot prove (avoid "nejdéle provozovaná", see Verification #2).

## 4. Concepts (8 s block = bars 29–32 at 129 BPM, 1 bar = 1,86 s; VO from 0,25 s to 5,75 s: "Velká ryba – vše na přívlač. Speciálka z Olomouce, kde si pruty Shimano vyzkoušíte přímo u vody.")

### A · "SHIMANO SPECIÁLKA" (gear-first, Japan-reel-ad grammar), **recommended**
Set: a deep navy studio (#0F2541 → #183C6B gradient), one cold rim light, and a faint oversized italic "PŘÍVLAČ" drifting behind the product. Uppercase Nunito 950 italic in white, and red #DA0F00 only on the single hit word.
1. **Bar 1 · "Velká ryba – vše na přívlač."** The H1 Vanquish rises out of the dark as a rim-lit silhouette. A specular sweep runs over the rotor (`fx.sweep`) and the kinetic headline **VŠE NA PŘÍVLAČ** reveals line by line on the words.
2. **Bar 2 · "Speciálka z Olomouce"** A macro push-in on the spool engraving "Vanquish CE" (`fx.pushIn`, magnification ≤ 1.0 of the 2k source). Glass chips count up: **145 g · 5,1 : 1 · 11 + 1**, captioned "Shimano Vanquish FA Competition Edition". A chip **OLOMOUC · OD 2011** lands on "Olomouce".
3. **Bar 3 · "kde si pruty Shimano vyzkoušíte přímo u vody"** A whip to S7: the Exlead rod draws diagonally across the frame with a light trail, over a duotone navy slice of the water plate (`plate_vanquish_box_water_1`). Chip: **SHIMANO ROD TEST CENTER**, and the kinetic hit word **U VODY**.
4. **Bar 4 · tag:** an H2 Cardiff flip (orange front → gold back, `fx.flip`) plus a 3-spoon mini fan (H3, S4, S1). Then **logo_reverse_on_navy** and **velka-ryba.cz**. The TA plate stays in its corner the whole time.
- Pros: it matches the VO exactly (rods, Shimano, water) and the shop's verified USP (Shimano Rod Test Center, premium end = Shimano). It is the **only hard-gear world in the film**, distinct from the spoon- and soft-bait-heavy sponsors (Troutshop, Activefishing, Moonky, Jigovky). The navy and white look reads as the shop's own brand.
- Cons: the heroes are Shimano products, so it could read as a Shimano ad. Mitigate with the Velká ryba logo or URL from bar 3 on, the headline "VŠE NA PŘÍVLAČ" being the shop's own claim, and no Shimano logo artwork beyond what is on the product.

### B · "TROUT AREA BOX" (colour-pop lure world)
The set switches colour fields on the beat, taken from the product accents: orange → pink → lime → navy. The **7-spoon fan** (H2, S4, S5, H3, S1, S2, S3) bursts out of an open lure box, with the Cardiff flip and the Illex macro. The headline **VŠE NA PŘÍVLAČ** lands as an outline-to-fill sweep. The Vanquish and the rod appear only in bar 4, on "pruty Shimano … u vody".
- Pros: very Reels-native and colourful, and squarely on topic for an MČR Trout Area.
- Cons: it **overlaps** with the other lure sponsors' worlds, and the shop's strongest differentiator (Shimano gear and the test center) gets one bar while the VO talks about rods.

### Recommendation
**A**, plus a **Trout Area hook** borrowed from B for the standalone Reel:
- **Reel hook (1 bar):** the H2 Cardiff spoon spins in on orange #FC9C24 → navy, with "TROUT AREA?" → "VŠE NA PŘÍVLAČ" (Nunito 950 italic) and a light sweep. This is a scroll-stopper in the most saturated colour of the set.
- **Block:** concept A as above.
- **End card (2 bars):** deep navy, `logo_reverse_on_navy` (or `logo_plate`), **velka-ryba.cz**, "Partner MČR v Trout Area 2026", and the TA logo, which is never smaller than the sponsor logo. An optional marquee shows "VŠE NA PŘÍVLAČ" in outline at 18 % opacity.
- **Layout:** keep text and logos inside x 64–1016, y 240–1460. In the right UI column (x > 940, y > 900) put no text, only the product's edge. The TA plate (64, 250, w 300) stays free: the headline starts below y ≈ 420, or to the right of the plate.

### Copy guardrails
- No "nejdéle provozovaná" (the shop's own superlative). "Od 2011" is safe.
- "30 let" refers to the people, not the shop. Do not use it in this block.
- Do not imply that Velká ryba makes or owns Shimano, Illex, Daiwa or Berkley. Write "u nás", "v nabídce" or "prodává", never "naše plandavky".
- Do not quote the Exlead's "nejcitlivější série … na trhu", which is the manufacturer's superlative.
- No prices on screen: they change and are not in the brief. No counts like "1 493 gumových nástrah" unless the operator approves them, since they are a snapshot dated 19 Oct 2026.
- Shimano Rod Test Center: the rental is at the **Olomouc store** (O nás). Keep "pruty si vyzkoušíte u vody" tied to the store, without promising locations.
